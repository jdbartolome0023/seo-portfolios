Investment Markets

Finance & investing content, brief-to-publish at volume

Australian investing/superannuation content site

Investment Markets' own writer produces the final copy, so my role sits earlier in the chain: the n8n pipeline and Claude Code generate the research and brief at volume, and I run quality and compliance review before anything reaches the writer.

What I owned

  • Brief production across 14 articles: super contributions, SMSF setup, retirement planning, investment risk
  • A fact-check and compliance pass on every brief before handoff
One review caught a Stage 3 tax-cut error (marginal rate cited as 30% instead of 32.5%, with the flow-on combined-rate and dollar-saving figures both wrong), plus a dead MoneySmart link. Both were corrected before the brief went to the writer, and a FAQ section and disclaimer were added.

No traffic data available for this account: my role here is upstream of publishing, not measured against it.
