Meta Ads: account takeover, audit-first, and a real attribution problem

WebProfits · Took over June 1, 2026 · €145/day approved envelope

I took over this account on June 1, 2026, and ran a full structural audit before making any changes: campaign setup, creative, audiences, pixel health, and budget efficiency across 7 campaigns. The most consequential finding wasn't a campaign problem, it was a tracking one.

Meta's pixel reported 75 purchases worth €20,793 in June. Cross-checking against WooCommerce's own order-level attribution found only 4 of those were genuinely traceable to Meta, worth €731: a ~19x gap in purchase count and ~28x in revenue.

Diagnosing and rebuilding a broken GA4 purchase-tracking pipeline

WebProfits · GTM-5Z97JLX · Diagnosed and rebuilt July 2026

On May 22, 2026, the client's web team removed a WordPress plugin without knowing it was the account's only GA4 "purchase" event source. Google Ads and GA4 both ran 8 weeks without real conversion data, invisible unless someone was actively checking event volume rather than just tag presence.

What I own

  • Traced the root cause back to the May 22 plugin removal by checking GA4's event stream against the site's actual tag inventory
  • Built and published a new GTM tag and trigger, firing purchase events directly off WooCommerce order data instead of the removed plugin
  • Run a recurring Profitability Check comparing GA4-tracked purchase value against real WooCommerce order exports, which is how I caught that even after the new tag went live, tracked values (€54.20 over 3 days) were still far below real revenue (€11,845.56 over the same window)
  • Escalated the remaining value-tracking gap to the client's dev agency with the exact discrepancy, rather than reporting the tag as fixed the moment it started firing

Full honesty: publishing the tag fixed the tracking blackout, events fire again, but it did not fully fix accurate value reporting. That gap is open and I track it against real WooCommerce revenue every reporting period until it closes.
